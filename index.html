Separate historical/archival significance from market price. Every score should be backed by evidence.

FactorWhat the app looks for
Temporal rarityHow early the first mint occurred relative to contract activity.
Supply scarcityOriginal mint quantity, not current holder count.
ProvenanceMint transaction, creator, and ownership history.
PreservationMetadata and artwork still accessible.
Collector interestListings, completed sales, bids, and independent attention.

🧾 Evidence & valuation policy

Verified facts

On-chain timestamps, token IDs, mint quantities, transaction hashes, creator wallet where determinable.

Research estimates

Antiquity score, historical importance, metadata quality, and scarcity category. These are assessments, not chain facts.

Market value

Use actual completed sales where possible. Floor prices and seller asks are not proof of fair value; thin markets can have no real liquidity.

Prototype v1 · Public read-only discovery + browser-local auction simulation. No wallet connection, custody, payments, smart-contract auction, or real marketplace listing is enabled. Never treat rarity scores or estimated values as investment advice.
